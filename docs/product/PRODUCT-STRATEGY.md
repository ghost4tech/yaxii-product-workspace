# Yaxii Product Workspace — Product Strategy

**Status:** Product authority

## Mission

Make WooCommerce product creation significantly faster, cleaner, and more pleasant than the normal Add Product workflow.

The Free edition is not a crippled demo. It should be a complete product-entry tool that proves Yaxii quality.

## Strategic goals

### Brand awareness

Use WordPress.org and worldwide WooCommerce distribution to introduce Yaxii to merchants, freelancers, and agencies.

In Algeria, use the product to help Yaxii become one of the first names people associate with serious WordPress/WooCommerce products.

### Quality demonstration

The desired reaction is:

> “If this is Yaxii's free plugin, their premium products are probably serious.”

### Leads

Turn users into Yaxii.dev visitors, documentation users, opt-in product-update subscribers, future Pro customers, users of other Yaxii products, and agency relationships.

### Revenue

Monetize advanced productivity instead of basic product creation:

- repetition;
- scale;
- advanced variations;
- safe batch work;
- AI assistance later;
- multi-store/agency operations later.

### Ecosystem expansion

The plugin broadens Yaxii beyond Algeria-specific COD workflows and can cross-sell into Smart Form, COD Theme, Confirm, Sheets, and future tools.

## Target users

### Free

- WooCommerce merchants adding products manually.
- Staff/power users entering products repeatedly.
- Freelancers building client stores.
- Agencies discovering Yaxii through catalog work.

### Pro

- Merchants with repetitive catalog structures.
- Variation-heavy stores.
- Agencies entering many similar products.
- Users doing batch catalog work.

### Agency later

- Agencies managing many client stores.
- Teams needing cross-store replication, approvals, scoped roles, and audit history.

## Positioning

**Create WooCommerce products faster without losing control.**

### Core differentiators

1. Modern workspace instead of the normal WooCommerce editor.
2. Save & Next.
3. Focus Mode.
4. Queue-backed operational feedback.
5. Keyboard-first productivity.
6. Strong mobile simple-entry experience.
7. Clean simple-to-variable workflow.
8. Later: templates and advanced variation productivity.

## Free / Pro decision rule

### Free

A feature belongs in Free when it is required to:

- create/edit an ordinary product correctly;
- avoid intentionally crippling the workflow;
- demonstrate the main Yaxii experience;
- make manual entry meaningfully faster;
- provide expected reliability, security, accessibility, localization, or recovery.

### Pro

A feature belongs in Pro when the job can already be completed without it, but the feature substantially improves:

- repetition;
- scale;
- batch productivity;
- advanced variation work;
- reusable workflows;
- AI/automation productivity.

### Agency

A feature belongs in Agency when its value mainly comes from:

- multiple stores;
- multiple clients;
- multiple operators;
- approvals;
- centralized operations;
- cross-store workflows;
- organizational audit history.

## Product principles

- Free must remain genuinely useful.
- No hard monthly limit on normal local product creation.
- Do not lock basic simple or variable CRUD.
- Security/capability enforcement is never premium.
- Do not build fake dashboards, fake AI, fake sync, or fake automation.
- Do not ship a feature before its state can be truthful.
- Prefer one excellent workflow over many shallow modules.
- WordPress.org trust and compatibility are first-class requirements.
- Future Pro extends the Free core instead of forking/rebuilding it.
